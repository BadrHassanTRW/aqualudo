Who we are

More than just an
academy.
We're a team.

Since 2017, Aqua Ludo has been the home of water sports in Cairo. From four coaches and a handful of boats, we've grown to 250 members, ten coaches, and thirty events a year. But the one thing that never changed: we still greet everyone who walks in with "Welcome — come on the water."

Our story

We started with 4 people
and two boats.

We were founded in 2017 by a group of rowers who wanted to share their love for the water with others. We grew into the place Cairenes come to when they want something real on the Nile.

We don't sell memberships just to sell. We sell them because we want to stay here, keep training, and keep meeting new people on the water.

Our values

What we
stand for.

Safety before speed.

Every session includes a life jacket, and every coach is trained in first aid. Our priority is always that you come off the water safe.

People before numbers.

We don't count members. We count the people who come back. And in truth, the numbers count themselves.

Enjoy while you learn.

Rowing isn't homework. Sport isn't punishment. We make training something you look forward to.

The Nile is our home.

We respect the place we train in, and we take care of the water. That's our duty.

Honesty in everything.

Our prices are clear, our times are clear, our expectations are clear. Nothing is hidden.

You improve every week.

We don't leave you where you are. With every session, you'll feel better than you were the week before.
